# This file contains the Scenarios and Stories

#------Scenarios------
#Joe hears about a local band performing at the venue.
# It's last minute and Joe wants to see all available seats and their positions so he can get the best seat possible.
# Joe wants to make sure he sees the cost of the seats when selected.
# Joe would also like a confirmation of the price at checkout.
# Joe wants his seat number included in his confirmation as well.

#Emily and her friends are going to see a new band play at the venue.
# Emily will order the tickets for the group ahead of time so she can choose the best seats for her group of four.
# Emily will use the grid style UI to select four seats for the group.
# She will be able to see the price of each seat when selecting one.
# Emily will add each seat to the cart one by one and at the end she will see the total price.
# Right before Emily goes to pay for the trip one of her friends bail on her.
# Emily decides she will go back to the selection screen and remove one of the seats from the cart.
# After removing the seat she will return to complete her transaction with the updated total.

#Rick is the current manager of the local music venue.
# The venue is small so Rick has to cancel any show that doesn't sell at least half of the seats.
# If the total number of seats sold is below half then Rick will see the app's suggestion to cancel the show.
# Rick also wants to check the totals from time to time of all seats sold.
# When the event starts, Rick wants to be able to view a map of the sold seats.
# Rick will use this map so he can act as security at the event.

#------Stories------
#Joe: As a last minute customer Joe wants to see all available seats when selecting his seat -
# - so he will get the best seat still available.
#Joe: As a younger guy who is broke Joe wants to see the price of any seat selected so he gets a good deal.
#Joe: As a forgetful guy Joe wants to see his seat number along with total price in a confirmation after purchasing -
# -so he gets the right seat.
#Emily: As the organizer for her group of friends Emily wants to add multiple seats to her cart-
# -so her checkout is simpler.
#Emily: As the organizer for her group Emily wants to be able to remove one of the seats so she-
# -doesn't waste her money when one of her friends bail.
#Rick: As the manager of the venue Rick wants to see all seats sold so he can kick out anyone who sneaks in.
#Rick: As the manager of the venue Rick wants to view a percentage of the seats sold easily -
# -so he can decide to cancel the show
#Rick: As the manager of the venue Rick wants to view the total made from the sales of the seats-
# -so he can make plans for future events.
