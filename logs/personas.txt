Personas:

Ticket Buyer: Joe is going to a concert by himself and wants to buy a ticket. Joe needs to be able to see available 
seats in a grid formation (letter/number), he needs to select a seat and see the seat he selected. He needs to be able to 
see the price of the seats available along with a confirmation to pay for the seat he selected.


Ticket Buyer: Emily is going to a concert with a group; she needs to be able to see available seats in a 
grid like UI text interface. She is buying multiple tickets, so she needs to be able to select multiple seats 
and see the individual prices for the seats as well as the total price of all selected seats. She wants to be able to clear 
her selection of seats if she decides to back out.


Manager: Rick is the venue manager. He wants to be able to see how many seats are sold for his venues show. Rick wants 
to know the percentage of the total seats that have been sold along with the total payout he can expect from the seats sold. 
He wants to know if 50% of the tickets have not been sold, in which case the show will not take place. The system should be able to
 tell rick whether he should go on with the show based on percentage of seats sold.
