Scenarios: 

Joe hears about a local band performing at the venue. It's last minute and Joe wants to 
see all available seats and their positions so he can get the best seat possible. Joe 
wants to make sure he sees the cost of the seats when selected and Joe would like a 
confirmation of the price at checkout. Joe wants his seat number included in his 
confirmation as well.

Emily and her friends are going to see a new band play at the venue. Emily will order 
the tickets for the group ahead of time so she can choose the best seats for her group 
of four. Emily will use the grid style UI to select four seats for the group. She will 
be able to see the price of each seat when selecting one. Emily will add each seat to 
the cart one by one and at the end she will see the total price. Right before Emily goes 
to pay for the trip one of her friends bail on her. Emily decides she will go back to 
the selection screen and remove one of the seats from the cart. After removing the seat 
she will return to complete her transaction with the updated total. 

Rick is the current manager of the local music venue. The venue is small so Rick has 
to cancel any show that doesn't sell at least half of the seats. If the total number 
of seats sold is below half then Rick will see the app's suggestion to cancel the show.  
Rick also wants to check the totals from time to time of all seats sold. When the event 
starts, Rick wants to be able to view a map of the sold seats. Rick will use this map 
so he can act as security at the event.




Scenario – Customer

Alex is a college student who enjoys going to concerts with his friends. When buying 
tickets, Alex wants to be able to see what seats are available so he can pick seats 
next to his friends. He also wants to be able to buy multiple tickets at once instead 
of having to buy them separately. Alex would like the system to show him the price of 
each ticket and the total cost before he decides to purchase them.

Scenario – Employee

Sarah is an employee who works at a small concert venue selling tickets to customers. 
She wants a system that keeps track of which seats have already been sold and which 
ones are still available. This would help prevent the same seat from being sold to 
multiple customers. Sarah also wants the system to automatically show the price of 
each seat depending on the row so she doesn't have to calculate it herself.

Scenario – Manager

Michael is a manager at a concert venue who is responsible for keeping track of ticket 
sales. He needs to know how many tickets have been sold and how much money the venue 
has made. Since the concert needs at least 50% of the seats to be sold, Michael wants 
a system that automatically calculates the percentage of seats sold. This would allow 
him to easily see if enough tickets have been purchased for the concert to take place.
